Mathematics here is split the way mathematical software conventionally splits
it, not the way the packages are. Where a function lives on npm is an
engineering detail; what it is _for_ is what you came looking for.

## Basic features

What the machine's own numbers can and cannot promise, and how to generate
numbers on purpose.

- **Floating point and tolerance** — why `0.1 + 0.2 !== 0.3`, what an _ulp_ is,
  and how to compare two results that should be equal but are not.
  (`@ac-kit/math-scalar`)
- **Random number generation** — fast seedable generators for simulation, and
  the separate, slower ones you must use for anything a person could attack.
  (`@ac-kit/math-random`, `@ac-kit/crypto-random`)

## Number representations

When binary64 is the wrong container.

- **Integers and modular arithmetic** (`@ac-kit/math-integer`)
- **Arbitrary precision, decimals and fractions** (`@ac-kit/math-numbers`)
- **Complex numbers** (`@ac-kit/math-complex`)
- **Algebraic structures** — groups, rings and fields as values you pass to an
  algorithm (`@ac-kit/math-algebra`)

## Mathematical functions

The named functions that statistics, physics and engineering are written in.
Everything in this group takes numbers and returns a number.

- [The gamma and beta functions](/node-essentials/topics/math/functions/gamma/)
  — the factorial, extended to every real number, and the ratio built from it
- [The error function](/node-essentials/topics/math/functions/error-functions/)
  — how much of a bell curve lies within a given distance of its centre
- [Incomplete gamma and beta](/node-essentials/topics/math/functions/incomplete-gamma-beta/)
  — cumulative distributions and the quantiles that invert them

## Numerical calculus

Answering a question about a function you can only evaluate, one sample at a
time. Everything in this group takes a _function_ and calls it repeatedly.

- [Root finding](/node-essentials/topics/math/calculus/root-finding/) — where
  does this cross zero?
- [Numerical integration](/node-essentials/topics/math/calculus/quadrature/) —
  what is the area under it?
- [Continued fractions](/node-essentials/topics/math/calculus/series/) — how do
  you evaluate an expression that never ends?
- [Convergence and iteration limits](/node-essentials/topics/math/calculus/convergence/)
  — how every routine here decides it is done, and what it does when it is not

## Structured spaces and applied areas

- **Linear algebra** — vectors, matrices and rotations (`@ac-kit/math-linear`)
- **Discrete grids** — lattices, tiling and pixel conversion (`@ac-kit/math-grid`)
- **Geometry** — shapes, coordinate systems and meshes (`@ac-kit/math-geometry`)
- **Curves** — Bézier curves, splines and arc length (`@ac-kit/math-curves`)
- **Statistics** — descriptive statistics, distributions and regression
  (`@ac-kit/math-stats`)
- **Combinatorics** — factorials, binomial coefficients and generators
  (`@ac-kit/math-combinatorics`)
- **Signal processing** — transforms, convolution, filters and windows
  (`@ac-kit/math-signal`)
- **Colour** — colour spaces, gamut mapping and perceptual difference
  (`@ac-kit/math-color`)
